THE STORY

一個由疲乏，走向喜樂的故事A journey from weariness to joy

這不只是一組聖誕詩歌，而是一個連貫的故事：渴望盼望的世界、神對救主的應許、耶穌的降生，以及基督為世界帶來的滿溢喜樂。
This is not simply a collection of Christmas songs. It tells one continuous story: a world longing for hope, God's promise of a Saviour, the birth of Jesus, and the overwhelming joy that Christ brings to the world.
